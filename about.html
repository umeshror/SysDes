Engineering Philosophy

The Values I Code By

Reliability as a Product Feature

Systems shouldn't just "work"—they should be resilient. I believe that 99.9% availability is a business requirement first and a technical challenge second. My designs prioritize self-healing and graceful degradation.

Simplicity Is Earned

The hardest systems to build are the ones that are simple to operate. I strive to remove unnecessary moving parts and focus on clear, documented architectural boundaries that teams can scale without friction.

Mentorship Through Design

Technical leadership isn't just about making decisions; it's about enabling others to make good ones. I use RFCs, design docs, and ADRs as tools for teaching and aligning the engineering organization.
